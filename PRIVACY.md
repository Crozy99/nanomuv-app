# NanoMuv Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 2026

NanoMuv ("the app", "the extension") is developed by an independent developer. This policy applies to both the NanoMuv Chrome extension and the NanoMuv Windows desktop application.

## The short version

NanoMuv does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. Everything it stores stays on your own device.

## What NanoMuv stores, and where

NanoMuv saves the following, entirely on your own device:

- **Your settings** — reminder interval, postpone length, exercise duration, theme preference, and which exercises you've enabled
- **Your stats** — completion streak, daily completed count, all-time total

In the Chrome extension, this is stored using the browser's built-in `chrome.storage.local` API. In the desktop app, this is stored in a local configuration file on your computer. In neither case does this information ever leave your device — there is no server, no account, and no synchronization with anything outside your own machine.

## What NanoMuv does not do

- It does not collect personally identifiable information
- It does not track your browsing activity, history, or the content of any web page or tab
- It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind
- It does not require or support user accounts
- It does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, because it does not collect any to begin with

## Permissions used (Chrome extension)

- **alarms** — to schedule your exercise reminders at the interval you choose, and to schedule the short delay if you postpone a reminder
- **storage** — to save your settings and stats locally, as described above
- **tabs** — solely to open a new tab showing the reminder screen when it's time for an exercise; NanoMuv does not read, modify, or access the content of any other tab

## Links to other sites

NanoMuv contains links to external sites you can choose to open — exercise demonstration videos on YouTube, cited research papers via their DOI links, and optional donation links (GitHub Sponsors, Ko-fi). Clicking these takes you to that third party's site, which has its own privacy policy. NanoMuv does not pass any of your data to these sites when you click a link — it's a standard outbound link, nothing more.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new effective date.
